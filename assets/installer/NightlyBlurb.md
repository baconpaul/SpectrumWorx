These are nightly builds of SpectrumWorx, a spectral effects plugin: it takes
the FFT of the incoming audio, hands the spectrum to a chain of up to eight
effect modules, and takes the inverse transform of what comes back.

Originally written by Little Endian Ltd. between 2010 and 2016, and revived
here as a CLAP-first plugin under the GPL. Formats: CLAP, VST3, AUv2 and a
standalone application, for macOS, Windows and Linux.

Download the file for your operating system and run the installer (macOS,
Windows) or unzip the plugins where your host looks for them (Linux).

**These are nightlies, not releases.** They are whatever the `main` branch
built this morning, they have not been through a release's testing, and the
preset format is not yet frozen. If one misbehaves, an issue with the host and
the operating system in it is worth more than a quiet uninstall.
