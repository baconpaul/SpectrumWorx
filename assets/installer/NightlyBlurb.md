These are nightly builds of SpectrumWorx, a spectral effects plugin.
Originally written by Little Endian Ltd. between 2010 and 2016, and revived
here as a CLAP-first plugin. Formats: CLAP, VST3, AUv2 and a
standalone application, for macOS, Windows and Linux. These binaries are AGPL-3-or-later.

**This port is currently still in a pre-release state. We may change many things still
and in some cases might make changes which are not compatible across versions. If you make
music with this you want to keep, we strongly suggest retaining a version or bouncing to stems
until a first numbered release.**
